Cliff Diving: High Dive Tour Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Cliff Diving: High Dive Tour ("the game") is made by Brano. This policy explains what data the game uses.

Data the game stores on your device. Your progress (stars, coins, unlocked divers and settings) is saved only on your phone. We do not have an account system and do not collect your name or email.

Advertising. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address) and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in the game. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Analytics and crash reports. We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played (for example, which venues are played and how dives score) and to fix crashes. This data includes app usage events, device model, OS version and an app instance ID. It is not used to identify you. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Facebook app events. If enabled, the Facebook SDK records app installs and launches so we can measure our ads. See https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings. Uninstalling the game removes all data stored on your device.

Contact. For questions, email the developer address listed on the game's Google Play page.
