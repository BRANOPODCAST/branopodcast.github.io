# Whistle: Referee Simulator

A football referee simulator for Android. Run with play, watch the challenge, then make the call: foul or fair, yellow or red, penalty or play on. Every decision is marked by the assessor, and the right calls take you from Sunday League pitches to the World Finals.

- Referee by the real Laws of the Game: fouls, cards, advantage, handball, offside and VAR reviews
- A career off the pitch: messages, a fan feed, sponsors and press conferences
- Handle the chaos: pitch invaders, flares, angry coaches and brawls
- Climb from Sunday League to the World Finals, with 24 achievements to unlock

Free to play with ads. No purchases. Works offline.

[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.soccermania.whistle)
